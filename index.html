Research

  • AquaPrecision / SoilSense-RL: physics-informed RL for precision irrigation

    In preparation

    Target: IEEE Sensors Journal

    Faculty: Prof. P. Jamdade, Prof. Dr. M. Bhoomkar

    • Built an OpenEnv-style Gym environment from scratch with soil-specific retention for six ICAR soil types, evapotranspiration-driven water balance and region-specific reservoir recharge.
    • Trained PPO agents for 400k timesteps per region. Over 100-episode evaluation, survival ranged from 73% (Thar) to 100% (Deccan and Himalayan foothills); ablations identified soil physics and the safety layer as the most critical components.
    • Developing a PINN + MobileNetV2 soil-photo model and an ESP32 device measuring moisture, pH, EC and temperature.

  • Bhu Kavach: real-time mine subsidence early warning

    NewManuscript prepared for IJAEOG, corresponding authorRepository private

    Led a five-member mechanical and computer science team. ESP32 + LoRa nodes reading tilt, strain and vibration over MQTT, a FastAPI backend with a WebSocket live feed, Random Forest risk scoring across Safe / Warning / Critical, a React dashboard and Telegram alerts. On simulated runs the Random Forest reached macro-F1 0.95 against 0.88 for fixed-threshold rules. Trained and tested on simulated data only, with field retraining pending.

  • Lifecycle-robust hybrid-electric propulsion

    Research paper drafted, targeting IEEE TAES

    A 60 kW turboshaft-plus-electric propulsion system for a fixed-wing UAV, coupled with a PINN-based digital twin for turbojet health monitoring. Full MATLAB chain: generator, rectifier, DC bus, inverter, propeller, energy-management controller and PSO optimisation. The baseline design verified feasible with a 26 kg fuel reserve and zero power deficit, and independent Python cross-validation caught two real bugs.

    HAL × IIT Indore AEROTHON 2026, Team Icarus, with U. Pawar, A. Deshpande, V. Memane·Jul 2026

    • MATLAB
    • PINN
    • Digital twin
    • PSO

About me

01 / Background

I am studying Mechanical Engineering at PVG COET in Pune, but my curiosity does not really stick to one subject. One day I might be exploring AI or working on a technical project; the next I could be editing a video or experimenting with web design.

I enjoy learning by doing, building things from scratch, and figuring out how seemingly unrelated interests come together.

02 / Research scope

My work sits at the intersection of machine learning, sensing and robotics. I am most interested in the point where a sensor reading stops being noise and becomes a decision, which is as much a physics problem as a modelling one.

In practice that has meant reinforcement-learning irrigation controllers grounded in soil physics, propulsion sized against engine degradation rather than a brand-new engine, and early-warning systems built on hardware that has to survive the field.

03 / Beyond the lab

I produce music, edit video, shoot photography and work in cinematography and graphic design. Editing turns out to use the same instinct as engineering: decide what the thing is for, then remove everything that is not doing that job.

Outside the technical work I organise (AIESEC, the Robotics Club and EDC at PVG), which is mostly the same problem of getting a lot of moving parts to arrive at the same place on time.
